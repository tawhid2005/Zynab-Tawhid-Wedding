# The Wedding of Zaynab & Tawhid 💍✨
### Blessed Nikah Ceremony — December 20, 2026

An animated, mobile-first luxury digital wedding invitation website built with HTML5, CSS3, Tailwind CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.

## ✨ Features
- **Interactive 3D Wax Seal Envelope:** Realistic paper opening animation with drop shadows and chime sound.
- **Background Wedding Music:** Plays Canon in D with floating audio player controls.
- **Royal Palace Courtyard:** High-res palace backdrop, falling rose petals particle animation, and rich floral border.
- **Dual-Mode Smart Timer:** Live countdown to the Nikah ceremony.
- **Interactive Schedule & Timeline:** Baraat, Nikah, Royal Feast, and Rukhsati with scroll-linked animations.
- **Venue & Google Maps Direction:** Direct GPS navigation link.
- **Add to Calendar (.ics):** One-tap sync with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook.
- **Social Media Link Thumbnail:** 1200x630 OpenGraph card preview for WhatsApp, Messenger, Facebook, and Telegram.
